# Contributing data to the pooled layer

**Draft — not yet in force.** Pooling is **off by default** and will not be switched
on until a lawyer has reviewed this agreement (see the
[design doc, §9.5](design/pooled-evidence.md)). This page is the plain-English
version of what contributing would mean, written for that review and for you.

Human Margin gets better when more advertisers can be compared against a shared,
anonymised picture of what is typical. You can help build that picture **without
revealing anything about your account**. Contributing is entirely optional.

## What you share

- **Only small, anonymised summary numbers**, computed **on your own machine** from
  **your own exports** (for example an Ads Manager CSV you downloaded). For a group
  such as "beauty, mid price band, Meta, this quarter": a few aggregate figures —
  typical CPM/CTR/CVR/ROAS ranges, a fatigue/decay direction, a sample count.
- A summary is shared **only when at least 5 different accounts** fall in the same
  group, so no figure can be traced back to one advertiser. Groups that are too
  specific to be safe are widened or dropped before anything is shared.

## What you never share

- No account name, page, handle, company name or ID.
- No ads, creatives, images, copy or targeting.
- No customer or personal data of any kind.
- No raw rows and no per-campaign numbers — only the group-level aggregates above.

Your data is never pulled from a platform on your behalf. You export your own data,
your own machine summarises it, and only the summary — above the 5-account floor —
is contributed.

## How it is used

- To form the "pooled similar accounts" ranges the tool shows alongside public
  benchmarks and your own numbers, always with how many accounts and how recent.
- Never to identify, rank or expose any advertiser, and never resold.

## Opting in and withdrawing

- **Opt-in:** nothing is contributed unless you explicitly turn it on.
- **Withdraw any time:** turning it off stops all future contribution, and your past
  contribution is removed from the aggregates when they are next recomputed.
- Because contributions are anonymised aggregates above the 5-account floor, no
  single past figure can be singled out and returned to you — withdrawal removes your
  input from the recomputation rather than reconstructing it.

## Status

This is a draft for legal review. Until that review is complete and the off-by-default
flag is turned on, no data is pooled and this agreement is not in force.
