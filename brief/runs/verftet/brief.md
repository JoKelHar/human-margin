## Brief

- **Objective:** Fill Verftet's spring course cohort with 250 sign-ups in the campaign window.
- **Audience:** Adults in Norway weighing an evening or weekend course to build a practical skill, who judge a provider on the instructor's credibility.
- **Insight:** Course sign-ups turn on trust in the teacher; a real instructor on camera carries proof an AI presenter cannot.
- **Proposition:** Learn from people who actually teach.
- **Maker:** human (cites verftet-pos) — Decide puts break-even at 16 of the 250 goal sign-ups, 6.4 percent of the goal, a bar a credibility-led course brand clears easily; at 1500 kr per sign-up the human shoot's trust is worth more than the saving.
- **Deliverables:** 1× course promo video (human); 3× instructor social clip (human)
- **Mandatories:** misleading; ai_act; ai_disclosure
- **Tone:** Credible, warm, practical.
- **Budget:** 40000 NOK
- **Success measure:** 250 sign-ups
- **Hypothesis:** A human-made creative set can fill the 250-sign-up spring cohort within budget, because its break-even over the AI route is only 16 extra sign-ups, 6.4 percent of the goal, which a credibility-led course brand should clear.
