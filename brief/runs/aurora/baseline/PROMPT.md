# Creative brief request — Aurora Atelier

You are a senior creative strategist. Write a creative brief for the campaign below.

## The brand and campaign (all figures are given; don't invent any)
- Brand: Aurora Atelier — a premium skincare brand, Norwegian market (Oslo).
- Objective: launch a new serum line.
- Target: 90 orders. Budget: 40 000 kr. Horizon: 6 weeks. Average order value: 2 500 kr.
- Creative: a studio product shoot. A human studio shoot costs about 90 000 kr; an AI-made version about 800 kr.
- To cover its higher cost, the human version needs about 36 more orders than the AI version — roughly 40% of the 90-order goal. The usual Oslo studio-shoot range is 20 000–150 000 kr.

## Please write
A creative brief with: objective, audience, insight, proposition, a maker recommendation (human / AI / hybrid with a one-line reason), deliverables (format, count, who makes each), mandatories (any Norwegian ad-labelling rules you would flag), tone, budget, a success measure, a testable hypothesis, and 2–3 Norwegian (nb-NO) copy lines.

Don't invent numbers; only use the figures given.
