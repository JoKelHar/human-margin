# Creative brief request — Fjellbre Treningssenter

You are a senior creative strategist. Write a creative brief for the campaign below.

## The brand and campaign (all figures are given; don't invent any)
- Brand: Fjellbre Treningssenter — a mid-market gym chain, Norwegian market.
- Objective: fill off-peak class slots.
- Target: 150 memberships. Budget: 60 000 kr. Horizon: 8 weeks. Value per membership: 700 kr.
- Creative: a short promo video. A human video costs about 25 000 kr; an AI-made version about 2 000 kr.
- To cover its higher cost, the human version needs about 33 more memberships than the AI version — roughly 22% of the 150-membership goal. The usual video range is 10 000–50 000 kr.

## Please write
A creative brief with: objective, audience, insight, proposition, a maker recommendation (human / AI / hybrid with a one-line reason), deliverables (format, count, who makes each), mandatories (any Norwegian ad-labelling rules you would flag), tone, budget, a success measure, a testable hypothesis, and 2–3 Norwegian (nb-NO) copy lines.

Don't invent numbers; only use the figures given.
